# Image Placement Guide

Place your render images and floor plans in the following locations:

## Hero Image
- `public/hero.jpg` - Main hero image (landscape, high resolution)
  - Recommended size: 1920x1080 or larger
  - Should be your best exterior or landscape shot

## Render Images (Gallery)
Place these in `public/renders/`:

1. `exterior-1.jpg` - Exterior view of the property
2. `interior-1.jpg` - Main interior space (dining/living)
3. `bath.jpg` - Bathroom or spa area
4. `bedroom.jpg` - Bedroom interior
5. `living.jpg` - Living room or lounge area
6. `view.jpg` - Landscape or mountain view

All images should be:
- High resolution (at least 1920px wide)
- JPG format for photos
- Landscape orientation preferred

## Floor Plans
Place these in `public/layouts/`:

- `floor-plan.png` - Main floor plan
  - PNG format (supports transparency)
  - High resolution for zoom capability
  - If you have DWG files, convert them to PNG first

## Converting DWG to PNG

Options for converting your DWG files:
1. AutoCAD: File > Export > PNG
2. DraftSight (free): File > Export > Image
3. Online converters: CloudConvert, Zamzar
4. QCAD (free, open-source CAD software)

Export settings:
- Resolution: 300 DPI or higher
- Background: White or transparent
- Size: At least 3000px wide for detail
